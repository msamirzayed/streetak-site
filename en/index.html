The courts

Six courts, each with its own mood.

  • Cairo rooftop

    Up on the roofs, with the whole city below you.

  • The alley

    Between the houses and the shops, under a wall that says «no football».

  • Under the pyramids

    Sand, night, and the pyramids behind the goal.

  • Space

    A match in orbit, with the Earth over your head.

  • The five-a-side cage

    Artificial turf and a chain-link fence, like the pitch down the road.

  • Neon Cairo

    A court of light in the rain, with a drone for a referee.
